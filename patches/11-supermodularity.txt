Rank 11: repair admission and measured interaction pilot

This addition lives in the overlay repository, not the upstream checker.

pilot/supermodularity/: finite executable repair model, eight laws, proofs,
and rejection fixtures.
tools/: strict supplied-evidence admission and exact interaction calculation.
tests/: acceptance, rejection, unresolved, schema, and checker-boundary regressions.
experiments/: reproducible CLI comparisons and real checker-guard repair evidence.
scripts/verify-supermodularity.sh: combined verification against the upstream pin.
.github/workflows/supermodularity.yml: CI execution and evidence retention.
docs/supermodularity.md: scope, commands, limitations, and measured results.

verify.sh now compares the checker against the pinned revision rather than only
the index. Staging a checker edit cannot hide it from the guard.

The finite model proves only its declared properties. External reports are
consistency-checked, not authenticated. The diagnostic experiment may report
zero or negative interaction; no positive result is required or assumed.
